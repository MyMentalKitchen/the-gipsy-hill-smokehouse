Privacy Policy

What we do with your information when you enquire.

Last updated 9 November 2026.

This policy explains what we do with your information when you contact us about catering. It is deliberately short, because we do very little with it.

Who we are

The Gipsy Hill Smokehouse Ltd, 121 Central Hill, London SE19 1BY. We are the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us at timclements@thegipsyhillsmokehouse.com or on 07944 390 309.

What we collect

When you fill in an enquiry form on this website we collect the details you type into it: your name, your email address, and — if you choose to give them — your phone number, the date of your event, the number of guests, the type of event, and whatever you write in the message box.

If you agree to cookies, we also use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read, and Google Ads to see whether people who clicked one of our ads went on to send an enquiry or call us. You can say yes to one and no to the other. See ‘Cookies’ below.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use it to reply to your enquiry, to prepare a quote, and — if you go ahead — to plan and deliver your event and keep in touch about it. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us about our services, and, once you book, the performance of our contract with you.

We will not add you to a mailing list off the back of an enquiry, and we will not sell or share your details with anyone for marketing.

We only use analytics and advertising cookies if you give your consent through our cookie banner, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

Who else sees it

Five suppliers handle your information on our behalf:

  • Formspree processes the website enquiry forms and passes the submission to us by email.
  • Google Workspace hosts our email, so your enquiry lands in a Gmail inbox.
  • GoDaddy hosts this website. Their systems keep standard server logs and run a performance-monitoring script on the pages they serve.
  • Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd): if you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics records which pages are visited, how people found the site, what kind of device they used and roughly where they are (town or region, never an address). It does not store your IP address. We see totals and trends, never who you are, and the data is kept for no more than 14 months.
  • Google Ads (Google Ireland Ltd): if you accept cookies, Google records whether a visit from one of our ads led to an enquiry or a phone call. We see totals only, never who you are. Google’s own use of this data is covered by its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

This site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

Cookies

When you first visit, we ask whether you’re happy for us to use analytics and advertising cookies. Nothing is set until you choose. Accept and Decline cover both; Manage preferences lets you choose each one separately.

Necessary. We store your cookie choice in your browser so we don’t ask you on every page. This is always on, because the site needs it to remember your answer.

Analytics (only if you accept). Google Analytics sets cookies (their names start with _ga) so it can tell one visit from the next. That shows us how many people visit, which pages they read and how they found us. They last up to two years. We use them only to see which parts of the site are useful, never to build a profile of you.

Advertising (only if you accept). Google Ads sets a cookie that tells us which of our ads led to an enquiry or a call. It lasts up to 90 days. We use it only to judge whether our advertising is working, never to build a profile of you.

If you decline, Google may still receive basic information without cookies (that a page was visited, with no way of identifying you), which it uses to estimate visit numbers and how our ads are performing.

You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

How long we keep it

If your enquiry does not become a booking, we keep the correspondence for up to two years, because people often come back to us a season or two later. If you do book, we keep the booking records for six years to meet our accounting and tax obligations. After that we delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to us holding it. Email us and we will deal with it within one month. There is no charge.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
