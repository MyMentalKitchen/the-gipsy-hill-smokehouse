Prices and Booking

What a hog roast or a barbecue costs with us, what's in the price, and how booking works.

How much does a hog roast cost?

From £1,350 to feed up to 100 guests. That includes a chef, a whole free-range pig, rolls, our homemade apple sauce and other sauces, and the cooking, carving and serving.

The minimum for a hog roast is 80 guests.

How much does a barbecue cost?

From £35 a head for a mixed-meat barbecue: steak, chicken and sausage, or three meats of your choice, with salads. That includes a chef; additional staff are extra.

The minimum for a barbecue is 10 guests.

How much does a lamb roast cost?

From £850 to serve up to 50 guests. The minimum for a lamb roast is 40 guests.

What's added to the price?

Extra staff, cleaning, and delivery and collection. Delivery is £45 and collection is £45, so £90 in all, within 50 miles of Gipsy Hill. Salads and sides are optional with a hog roast.

We itemise every quote, so you can see what you're paying for.

Why does every quote come out different?

Because every menu is put together for the event. The price moves with what you pick, how many guests are coming, how many staff you need and how long we're serving for, and we work to your budget. The prices above are where a hog roast and a barbecue start.

What are the minimum numbers?

10 guests for a barbecue, 40 for a lamb roast and 80 for a hog roast.

How far ahead should I book?

Ideally several months ahead, so your RSVPs and your guests' dietary needs are in before we plan the food. If you're short of time, ask anyway: depending on the date and the menu, we can sometimes cook the next day.

When can I call you?

Any day, 8am to 9pm, on 07944 390 309.

How does booking work?

  1. Send us your date, your numbers and your postcode, with the form or on 07944 390 309. We come back to you within 24 hours.
  2. We put the menu and the quote together with you.
  3. A 50% deposit by bank transfer confirms the booking.
  4. Final numbers by email at least 10 working days before the event.
  5. The balance is paid 10 days before the event.

The full terms are on our terms and conditions page.
