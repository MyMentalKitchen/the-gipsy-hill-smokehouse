## 2. UNKNOWN — must be answered before appearing anywhere

**Blocking (site cannot ship correctly without these)**
1. **Formspree plan + current month's submission count.** Free tier caps monthly submissions; one bot run swallows the allowance and real enquiries drop silently with no bounce. Every form on the site points at `mqeryylv`. Answer before launch, add spam protection, and add a fallback delivery path.
2. **Legal entity + company number** for the footer. Not on the fact sheet.
3. **VAT registered?** Determines whether "from £25 per head" reads inc or ex VAT.
4. **Privacy policy content + cookie position.** The site collects name, email, phone, event date and venue address. No privacy policy exists. UK GDPR requirement, not a nice-to-have.
5. **The actual T&Cs document** — referenced by the deposit line, not supplied, and there is no page to link to.

**Commercial detail**
6. **£1.92/mile — from where, beyond what point, one way or return?** Unusable as published without this.
7. **Does "from £25.00 per head" include service, staff, travel, equipment?** What does £25 actually buy?
8. **Pricing for nationwide/abroad work.** Stated as available; no charge basis given.
9. **What "Hog Roast 100 / Hog Roast 150 / Barbeque / From The Sea" mean.** These are live options in the enquiry form dropdown with nothing on the site explaining them.
10. **Practical minimum.** Fact sheet says no minimum; the spit roast page says lamb "serves up to 50" and a leg of lamb "serves 10". What does a 20-guest booking actually look like?

**Operational detail behind one-word answers**
11. **Wet-weather contingency = what, specifically?** Gazebo, covered cooking area, client-supplied marquee? "Yes" is not publishable.
12. **3m × 3m — of what?** Machine footprint, service area, or both. Plus: power required? Gas? Level ground? Access width for the machine? Overhead clearance/outdoor only?
13. **"Cater to all needs" — the actual provision.** Named vegetarian and vegan options, allergen handling, halal (the spit roast page already claims halal chickens to order — is that current?).
14. **Enquiry hours vs service hours.** Mon–Sat 8.30am–10pm: is that when you answer the phone, or when you'll cater? No Sunday listed, but Sunday weddings and parties are core business.
15. **Response-time commitment.** The live site promises "within 24 hours" on five pages. Confirm you will hold it or the promise comes off.

**Proof assets**
16. **Google Business Profile URL.** Field says "Claimed" — the URL is still needed for the contact page and for schema.
17. **Which named Google reviews go on the site, and in what form.** Fact sheet says named testimonials are available; nothing has been selected.
18. **Public liability insurance level + food hygiene rating.** Wedding venues and corporate procurement ask for both; neither is on the fact sheet.
19. **Photo shortlist.** "iCloud and Google Photos" is a location, not a selection. Need ~30 chosen images, plus permission for any identifiable guests, couples or venues.

## 5. Primary conversion goal

**One qualified enquiry, submitted through the form, carrying event date + guest count + venue/postcode + event type.**

Everything else is secondary. Rationale:
- Every service on the site is quoted, not bought — there is no transaction to complete online.
- Date and guest count are what make an enquiry answerable; an enquiry without them costs a round-trip before you can price it.
- The form is the only channel that lands in a trackable place. A phone call at 8pm on a Saturday service does not.

Supporting actions, ranked: **call 07944 390 309** (secondary, for people who won't fill a form) → **view menus** (the main pre-enquiry step) → **read Google reviews** (proof, off-site).

**Two conditions on this goal:**
1. The Formspree cap question (§2.1) must be answered first. A conversion goal routed through a channel that can silently stop delivering is not a goal, it's a leak.
2. The form must capture the qualifying fields on every page it appears — the enquiry forms currently differ between the contact page and the corporate page.
