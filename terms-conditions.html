The terms that apply to catering bookings with us.

Last updated 21 August 2026. These terms apply to catering bookings with The Gipsy Hill Smokehouse Ltd.

1. Confirmation of booking

1.1 Bookings are only regarded as confirmed upon receipt of a deposit.

2. Deposits, pre-payments and settlement of accounts

2.1 The Customer is required to pay a deposit of 50% to The Gipsy Hill Smokehouse Ltd in order to confirm the function. This deposit is payable on confirmation of the booking. Payment is to be made by bank transfer.

2.2 Any outstanding charges must be paid in full 10 days before the event.

2.3 Any queries concerning the account, or any complaints, must be submitted in writing within 7 days of the date stated on the invoice.

3. Final numbers

Final numbers for any function must be confirmed by email at least 10 working days before the date of the event. Should the number in your party decrease, we require 5 working days' notice, failing which a charge will be made for the original number of diners. Similarly, 5 working days' notice is required for any additional diners.

4. Cancellation by The Gipsy Hill Smokehouse

The Gipsy Hill Smokehouse Ltd may cancel a booking without incurring any liability if:

4.1 The venue is closed due to events or circumstances beyond its control, such as fire, water leakage, or by order of a public authority.

4.2 The Customer becomes insolvent or enters into liquidation or receivership.

5. Cancellation by the Customer

5.1 All cancellations by the Customer must be made in writing to the Events Manager.

5.2 Events of 50 people or more. Cancellations more than 3 months before the event — no charge. Cancellations between 3 months and 1 month — 25% deposit. Cancellations 1 month or less before the event — 100% of minimum spend.

5.3 Events of fewer than 50 people. Cancellations not less than 14 days before the event — no charge. Cancellations between 14 and 7 days — 25% deposit. Cancellations 7 days or less before the event — 100% of minimum spend.

6. Prices

6.1 The Customer agrees to the minimum payment and maximum guest numbers set out in their booking confirmation. This charge includes VAT, food and beverage, and any additional services or equipment hire agreed.

6.2 All prices are current at the time of quoting and are inclusive of VAT or other government taxes where appropriate. The Gipsy Hill Smokehouse Ltd reserves the right to amend prices to take account of any change in the rate of VAT or other taxes.
