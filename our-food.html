BBQ Catering, Cooked Over Fire

BBQ catering cooked over fire at your venue: smoked brisket and ribs, piri piri chicken, sardines, salads and sides, with the best of British produce.

We bring the fire to you. The marinade goes on the night before. The fire is lit hours before your first guest arrives. The meat comes off when it's ready, not when it's convenient.

Then we cook, serve the salads and sides, and take the equipment away when we're done.

You may know us from the market

You may have known us from Borough Market or Broadway Market.

We now operate as private caterers, catering for weddings, private parties and corporate events.

However, we love catering rugby festivals and one-day events too.

How we run a wedding is on our BBQ wedding catering page.

Smoked BBQ catering: what goes on the fire

We smoke beef brisket and spicy pork ribs over ash and Irish peat.

Portuguese cooking has always shaped our style. Our first barbecues were loins of pork, chicken piri piri and sardine parties. Brazilian barbecues came later.

It's the American and the Portuguese ends of the same fire. Every dish is on our BBQ catering menu, with a picture of each.

BBQ Chicken

Corn-fed free range chickens marinated with lemon and piri piri.

BBQ Roast Pork

Free range loins of pork marinated with fennel, rosemary and garlic.

Spit Roast Lamb cooked over wood

Lamb on the spit over wood, served into wraps with minty yoghurt and redcurrant jelly.

Beef Ribs

Beef ribs off the barbecue, alongside the tomahawk, rib eye, sirloin and picanha.

Beef Brisket

Beef brisket, smoked over ash and Irish peat.

Barbecue Sardines

Sardines on the barbecue, as at the sardine parties we've run through the summer.

Contains fish.

Salads & Sides

Hot new potatoes with herby butter and roasted Mediterranean veg, next to cold salads: Greek salad, coleslaw and dressed green leaves, with rustic bread and marinated olives.

The hot sides go out hot. The leaves are dressed in the last few minutes, never before, because dressed leaves collapse. You choose which go with the meat.

BBQ catering questions

Are you BBQ caterers near me?

Yes, if you're in London or the South East. We're based in Gipsy Hill, SE19, and cook at homes and venues across London and the South East, and travel further for special occasions.

Can we have a whole hog on the spit as well?

Yes. Some parties want more than one thing on the fire. Here's how our hog roast catering in London works.

Can you cater for allergies and dietary needs?

Yes. We plan dietary needs guest by guest, so tell us about any allergies or dietary needs when you get in touch. Halal chickens are roasted to order, and there are vegetarian dishes on the menu: halloumi kebabs, vegetarian burgers and vegetarian tartlets.

How much does BBQ catering cost?

From £35 a head for a mixed-meat barbecue: steak, chicken and sausage, or three meats of your choice, with salads, for 10 guests or more. The final price depends on how many guests you have, which dishes you pick, how many staff you need and how long we're serving for. Every quote is worked out for the event. See prices and booking.

Tell us about your barbecue

Send us your date, your numbers and your postcode, and tell us about anyone with an allergy before we plan the menu, not after. We'll come back to you within 24 hours and tell you how we'd cook it.
