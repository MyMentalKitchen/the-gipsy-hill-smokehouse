Hog roast hire in London

We bring a whole free-range hog to your garden, venue or office, cook it on the spit through the day and carve it in front of your guests. With us, hog roast hire means the hog, the fire, the equipment and the people to run it, from the moment we unload to the moment we've cleared up.

We're in Gipsy Hill, SE19, and we've been cooking hogs over fire for events since 2004. From 2005 we also traded as Roast Hog at Borough Market, and we were the hog roaster at Broadway Market from the day it started until 2019.

The hog

Free-range, from Blythburgh Farm in Suffolk. We marinate it porchetta-style in fennel, rosemary, garlic and spices in extra virgin olive oil, then roast it on the spit for at least five to six hours.

Before it goes on, we score the skin by hand in a herringbone pattern. On a whole hog that takes about 25 minutes. It's why the crackling lifts evenly across the ribs and not in patches. How we get the crackling right.

We carve it at the fire and serve it in rolls with rocket, home-made apple sauce and a piece of crackling.

What happens on the day

The hog goes on the fire early. At a wedding this August, for 95 guests, it went on at eight in the morning and came through the marquee whole at five to four. So if you want to eat at lunchtime, we'll be with you first thing.

We bring the fire and the spit, the cooking and serving equipment, the tables, and the chefs and waiting staff. Tell us about the space when you get in touch and we'll tell you how we'd set up in it.

We plan dietary needs guest by guest. A hog roast isn't halal, so for halal guests we roast halal chickens alongside it, and there are vegetarian dishes on the menu.

How much does a hog roast cost in London?

It depends on four things: how many guests you have, which salads and sides you pick, how many staff you need and how long we're serving for. Every quote is worked out for the event.

A good share of any quote is the equipment, the staff hours, the cleaning and getting there and back, not just the food. We itemise it, so you can see what you're paying for.

Send us your date, your numbers and your postcode and we'll come back to you within 24 hours.

Where we cook

Hog roasts for weddings, parties and work events

The hog is the same. How we serve it changes. At a wedding we can carve it onto sharing platters for each table, run a buffet at the fire, or do rolls for the evening. At a birthday in the garden it's usually rolls from the fire through the afternoon.
